What Lot & Loaded is

Lot & Loaded originates off-market commercial property — industrial land, redevelopment and teardown sites, warehouses, and outdoor storage yards — and matches it with vetted builders and developers. Joining is free for builders, partners, and sellers. Lot & Loaded is not a real estate brokerage, and nothing on this site is legal, tax, or investment advice. Every party should use its own counsel on its own paperwork.

How a transaction works

When a property fits, the seller signs one contract with Lot & Loaded at an agreed price. Lot & Loaded assigns that contract to a vetted builder from its bench, and the builder closes the purchase — cash, as-is, on the date in the contract. The assignment is disclosed in the paperwork the seller signs. Lot & Loaded earns an assignment fee inside the transaction at closing; the fee never reduces the seller's agreed price and is never billed to builders, partners, or sellers. If no transaction closes, no fee is earned.

Sellers

Sending the seller form is not a contract and obligates you to nothing. Any number we discuss is an offer, not a commitment, until a written contract is signed by both sides. Every closing is subject to the signed contract's own terms, including title.

Builders

Qualification is our judgment, based on closing pace and fit with the parcels we originate. Qualification does not guarantee any volume of deals, and a parcel shown to you may be shown under a stated first-look clock. There are no membership, seat, or per-deal charges for builders.

Partners

Register a parcel before first contact and it is locked to you for 30 days from submission. During the lock we do not work that parcel around you. Partners keep 50% of the net assignment fee actually collected on their deals, paid when the fee is collected. Partner work is 100% commission — no salary, hourly pay, or draw is offered or implied. Unregistered leads are not protected.

How pricing works

Parcels are priced off recorded sales of comparable property in the same market — what we call builder value: what a builder credibly pays for property of that kind, from the record. Our contract target is 65% of that figure. The methodology describes how we price; it is not an appraisal, not a valuation opinion, and not a promise of what any property will sell or appraise for.

Communications

When you send a form or contact us, we may reply by email, phone, or text about your property, your registration, or your buy box. Message and data rates may apply on texts. Tell us to stop and we stop — one word is enough, and it is honored across every channel we run.

Partner conduct

Partners work owners directly, by hand, on their own phones and their own accounts. Automated bulk outreach to property owners is not part of this platform and is not authorized in our name. A partner who misrepresents the model — including describing an assignment as a direct purchase by Lot & Loaded — loses the lock and the registration.

Changes

These terms may change as the platform grows. The version on this page is the current one; a signed contract is governed by its own terms, not by this page.

No guarantees

We do not guarantee that a property will sell, that a deal will close, or that any timeline will hold. Pricing figures on this site describe our methodology — parcels priced off recorded comparable sales — not a promise about any individual property.
