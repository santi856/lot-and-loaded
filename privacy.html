Privacy

What we collect, and what we never do with it.

Last updated October 26, 2026.

What we collect

Only what the forms on this site ask for: your name, email, and phone number, and the property details you choose to send — address, acreage, zoning, asking price, and your notes about the property. This site also uses Google Analytics, which collects standard visit data (pages viewed, device, approximate location) through cookies.

Why we collect it

To run our process: verify the property, price it against recorded comparable sales, match it to qualified builders, and contact you about it. Builder packets contain property details a builder needs to evaluate a deal. Your contact details are shared only as needed to work and close a deal you are part of.

What we never do

We never sell your information. We never share it outside the deal process described above. We do not put you on marketing lists because you sent a form.

Property information vs. personal information

A deal packet is about a property, not a person. It carries the parcel's address, acreage, zoning, recorded comparable sales, and our pricing math. It does not carry a seller's phone number, email, or private notes — those stay in our intake system and are used only to work the deal with the seller directly. When a builder takes a deal, the closing paperwork is handled by the title company under its own privacy practices.

Information from public records

Commercial parcels are originated from public records — county property appraiser and tax rolls, recorded deeds, and court records. Those records are public by law. We use them to identify parcels and the entities that own them, and to verify acreage, zoning, tenure, and tax status before a parcel is ever offered to a builder. If you are an owner and want to know what public-record information we hold on your parcel, ask — we will show you, and the contact details are below.

What we do not collect

We never ask for, and you should never send us, bank account details, Social Security numbers, or payment card information through this site. Nothing is bought or paid for on this site. Fees move only inside a closing, through the title company, under the signed paperwork.

How it is stored

Form submissions are stored in our intake database, access-limited to the people working your deal. We keep a submission while a deal, registration, or conversation is active, and for a reasonable period afterward for our records. Analytics data is held by Google under Google's own policies.

Texts and calls

If you give us a phone number, we may call or text that number about the property, registration, or buy box you contacted us about. Reply STOP to any text and texting stops. Your number is never sold and never added to bulk campaigns.

Children

This is a business platform for commercial property. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. The version here is the one that applies.
