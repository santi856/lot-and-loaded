Our standard: quality and time

We function on quality and time, and that's what we give people. Sellers get a fast, certain close. Partners get paid when deals close. So every builder in this community is qualified on closing pace before a single deal goes out — a slow close ties up everybody's money, and we don't do that to our sellers or our partners.

How it works

  1. 1. Deals are originated owner-direct

    Our partners work owners directly — no daisy chains, no recycled lists. Every parcel is verified: zoning, acreage, ownership.

  2. 2. Every parcel passes a full gate

    Criteria fit, numbers, recorded comps. We price at 65% of what a builder credibly pays for the dirt — never off a seller's ask.

  3. 3. Qualified builders get the drop

    Deals go only to builders whose box they fit, fastest closers first, on a stated clock. Clean packet: the deal, the numbers, the timeline.

Questions before you start? The builder form takes about three minutes and asks only what we need to match you with the right parcels. Partner registration takes two.

What we buy

Development land, warehouses and industrial buildings, teardown and redevelopment sites, and industrial outdoor storage yards. Our industrial lane works sites of five acres and up; other commercial parcels are reviewed on fit. Everything is originated owner-direct and off-market — we do not work fresh listings, and we do not touch daisy chains. If a parcel cannot be verified — zoning, acreage, ownership — it does not go out to builders. If it can, it is priced off recorded comps and matched to the builders whose box it fits.

What it costs

Free for builders, partners, and sellers — no membership, seat, per-lead, or per-deal charges, ever. The house earns one way: a fee inside the contract assignment when a deal closes. If a deal does not close, we do not earn, and nobody owes us anything for looking.

How an offer is made

Once a parcel clears the gate, we take it to qualified builders whose box it fits — fastest closers first, on a stated first-look clock. Terms are agreed up front: the assignment, earnest money, and the closing date. If a builder passes, we say so and move to the next fit. When a contract is signed, title runs the assignment and the clock is watched all the way to the closing table. We work Florida first, where our builder bench is deepest, and we take on new markets builder by builder — never by spraying listings.

Questions, answered straight

What does Lot & Loaded do?

We originate off-market commercial parcels — industrial land, redevelopment and teardown sites — and put them under contract at 65% of builder value. Qualified builders see the deals that fit their box, partners register parcels with the house, and owners can sell to us directly.

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. There are no membership fees for builders, partners, or sellers. We make money when deals close — our fee is inside the contract assignment — so we only earn when you do.

How are parcels priced?

Every parcel passes a full gate: criteria fit, numbers, and recorded comps. We price at 65% of what a builder credibly pays for the property — never off a seller's asking price.

Who sees the deals?

Only qualified builders whose buy box the parcel fits. Builders are qualified on closing pace before any deal goes out, and the fastest closers see fitting deals first, on a stated clock.

I'm a wholesaler — how do I work with you?
