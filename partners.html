How a registration works

One: register the parcel before first contact — the lock starts at submission and runs 30 days. Two: HQ runs the full gate: criteria fit, the numbers, recorded comps, and a match against qualified builders' buy boxes. Three: the house prices the offer — no offer goes to a seller from anyone until that happens. Four: the deal goes to qualified builders, fastest closers first, on a stated clock. Five: when the assignment fee is collected, your 50% is paid. Registration is free, and it is the only way a lead is protected — unregistered leads are first come, first served.

What counts as a lead

The property address, the owner's name — the signer, not a gatekeeper — and a skip-traced phone that reaches them. Acreage and zoning help the gate move faster. Listed deals need 90 or more days on market. Hand-worked outreach only: your own phone, your own accounts.

What HQ does for free

Skip-tracing the owner, running the recorded comps, pricing the offer, writing the outreach, working every reply, and matching the deal to qualified builders — the house does all of it, on every registered lead, at no cost to you. Your job is finding parcels worth registering: off-market, owner-direct, with the owner reachable. Where to find them: tax-delinquent industrial parcels, long-held family land, estates with out-of-state heirs, and owners paying carrying costs on vacant warehouses that no longer serve them. If a parcel is already under contract with another wholesaler, or the house is already working the owner, we tell you at registration — not after you have spent a week on it.

Questions, answered straight

How does the 30-day lock work?

Register a parcel before first contact and the lead is locked to you for 30 days, starting the moment you submit. During your lock we do not work that parcel around you — if the fee lands on it, your split lands with it. Unregistered leads are not protected.

When and how do I get paid?

The assignment fee is typically 5% of the contract price. You keep 50% of the net fee actually collected on your deals, paid when the fee lands. It is 100% commission — no salary, no hourly, no draw.

Can I send the seller an offer myself?

No. HQ runs the full gate — criteria fit, numbers, recorded comps, buyer match — and prices every offer. No offer goes to a seller until the house runs it, from anyone.

What makes a lead count?

The property address, the owner's name, and a skip-traced phone that reaches the owner. A lead without the owner and a phone is not a lead yet — get them first, then register.

What kinds of parcels fit?

Off-market commercial and industrial property: development land, warehouses and industrial buildings, teardown and redevelopment sites, and outdoor storage yards. Listed deals need 90 or more days on market.
