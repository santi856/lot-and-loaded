How qualification works

We review every submission by hand. The grade turns on pace: closings in the last 12 months, typical days-to-close once terms are agreed, and how fast you answer when a deal fits. Funds matter too — we verify proof of funds before any contract is assigned, and earnest-money behavior tells us how a close will actually run. What you build and where you buy decide which parcels you see; a tight box gets better deals than a wide one. Builders who close fastest see fitting parcels first, on a stated first-look clock. If a deal cannot be performed on at pace, it is not a fit — no hard feelings either way. Nothing you share is sold or shared outside our deal process.

What you receive

A parcel packet with the facts the gate verified: acreage, zoning, access, and recorded comp support, with the price and its basis shown. No inflated values and no dressed-up projections — the numbers on the packet are the numbers the price was built on. You get a decision window that matches the pace you stated at qualification, and a straight answer when the clock runs out; deals are re-offered, not sat on.

After you say yes

Terms are locked in writing before anything is assigned: price, earnest money, closing date. Title runs the assignment, and our fee is inside it. Miss the date without a signed extension and the parcel goes to the next qualified builder.

Questions, answered straight

How long does qualification take?

The form takes about three minutes. We review every submission by hand and grade it on closing pace and fit — there is no instant approval, because sellers are promised a fast, certain close.

What do you qualify on?

Closing pace, first of all: your closings in the last 12 months, your typical days-to-close, and how fast you answer when a deal fits. We also look at what you build, where you buy, your funds and proof of funds, earnest-money behavior, and who decides on a site.

What does it cost?

Nothing. No membership, seat, or per-deal charges for builders — ever. We make money on the deal side when a contract closes.

How are deals presented?

As a clean packet: the property, the numbers, and the timeline, with a first-look window. Deals go only to builders whose box they fit, fastest closers first.

Do you close on assignments?

Yes. We originate by contract assignment and our fee is inside the assignment; title runs it. We verify proof of funds before any contract is assigned.
