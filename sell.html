What we look at

Recorded sales of similar property near yours, the zoning and what can actually be built, acreage and road access, utilities, and what the builders we work with are buying right now. We do not price off tax assessments or online estimates. If your property has environmental questions or title issues, tell us in the notes — surprises found later slow everything down; facts up front usually don't. Inherited property, property behind on taxes, and property held by partners who no longer agree on it are all situations we see; tell us the situation and we will work with it. If we agree a number, the contract is plain: cash, as-is, and a closing date inside a fast window that you agree to. No open houses and no strangers walking your property.
